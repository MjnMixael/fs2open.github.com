Music Player

Opens via View › Music Player.

Plays back music files from the game's music folder while you work, so you can audition tracks without launching FreeSpace Open. Useful when choosing tracks for Mission Specs, Briefing, Debriefing, or Command Briefing.

Nothing is selected when the editor starts. To play music, open the dialog and select a track, then press Play or double-click the track. Every button is disabled unless it can do something right now.

The right end of the main toolbar has a box of controls for the track that is loaded (playing or paused), so you can work with the dialog closed: the music keeps playing when you close it. The first button in the box names the loaded track; click it to open the dialog. The toolbar can pause, resume, stop and skip the loaded track, but it can't start a track, so its buttons are disabled while nothing is loaded. Autoplay is set in the dialog, and keeps working with the dialog closed. To hide the toolbar controls, turn off Music Player controls on the toolbar in Preferences. With them hidden, closing the dialog stops the music.

When you open the dialog again, the loaded track is selected, or nothing if no track is loaded.

Controls

ControlDescription
Track listAll music files found in the game's music folder. Click a track to select it, or double-click it to play it right away.
Play / PauseIn the dialog, plays the selected track, or resumes it if it is the paused one. On the toolbar, pauses or resumes the loaded track. While music plays, the button turns into Pause.
StopStop the music and unload the track.
Previous / NextMove to the previous or next track in the list. With a track loaded, they step from it and the new track plays; otherwise they move the selection.
AutoplayWhen enabled, playback advances automatically to the next track when the current one ends, like a playlist.
Music.tblShow the music table data.
